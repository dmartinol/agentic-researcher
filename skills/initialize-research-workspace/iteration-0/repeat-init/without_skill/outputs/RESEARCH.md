# Research

## Topic

Cloud migration cost optimization strategies

## Objective

What are the most effective strategies for reducing costs during a cloud migration project?

## Scope

### Included

- AWS, Azure, and GCP migration strategies
- Cost optimization techniques
- Migration timeline impacts on costs
- Resource right-sizing approaches

### Excluded

- On-premise infrastructure costs
- Multi-cloud strategies
- Specific vendor pricing

## Expected Outputs

- Comparative analysis report
- Cost optimization recommendations
- Migration timeline impact analysis

## Evidence Policy

- Prefer authoritative and primary sources where available.
- Record provenance for important factual claims.
- Preserve conflicting credible evidence.
- Do not infer absence from lack of evidence.
- Revalidate evidence when freshness materially affects the conclusion.

Additional research-specific evidence requirements:

- Sources must be from last 2 years
- Vendor documentation preferred for cost models
