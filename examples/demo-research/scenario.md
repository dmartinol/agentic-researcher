# Scenario: MCP aggregation architecture

## Research question

Should a cloud-native AI platform introduce a centralized MCP aggregation/gateway layer rather than connecting agent clients directly to individual MCP servers?

## Scope

Compare the approaches for discovery/tool aggregation, authentication/authorization boundaries, policy/governance, operational complexity and failure domains, observability, portability, and vendor coupling.

Exclude implementation scheduling, staffing, budget, and product procurement.

## Evidence expectations

Prefer current protocol specifications, official product/project documentation, source repositories, and other primary technical material. Use independent sources where they materially validate or challenge a conclusion.

For conclusion-critical claims preserve provenance, check version/date applicability, actively search for contradictory or qualifying evidence, and leave unresolved disagreement visible.

## Expected plan shape

Create multiple independent Research Tasks where practical, followed by a dependent Synthesis Task. The plan remains provider-neutral even when concrete providers are configured.

## Expected outputs

Produce a concise architecture comparison with evidence-backed findings, material conflicts/unknowns, and implications of each approach. Do not force a binary recommendation when evidence depends on deployment context.

## Demo-specific checks

Demonstrate setup/plan approval gates, idempotent external initialization, canonical claim/source memory, explicit contradiction search, selective retrieval, fresh-session resumption, dependent synthesis, read-only verification, and explicit approval before closure.
